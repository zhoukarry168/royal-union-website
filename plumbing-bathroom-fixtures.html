How we manage the category

A sourcing route built around
technical confirmation.

The objective is not to move fastest at the quotation stage. It is to make sure the supplier, sample and production order are being compared against the same brief.

  1. 01

    Define the application

    Share product photos, drawings, dimensions, material, quantity and destination.

  2. 02

    Compare suppliers

    Review product fit, specification basis, packaging and quotation conditions.

  3. 03

    Approve the sample

    Confirm measurements, appearance, connection and agreed reference before production.

  4. 04

    Control the order

    Coordinate production follow-up, checks, receiving, consolidation and delivery preparation.

What to confirm
before you order.

These answers explain the status of the online catalogue and the information needed to start a plumbing product request.

Ask about your requirement →
What products are included in Plumbing and Bathroom Fixtures?

The category is organized into sewage pipe products, faucets and drain pipes. The online catalogue includes 12 sewage pipe products, 41 faucet products and 7 drain pipe products.

Can ROYAL UNION source custom plumbing specifications?

We can coordinate supplier research and quotation against required material, dimensions, connection type, finish, packaging and destination-market requirements. Feasibility must be confirmed for each request.

Are the online product specifications final?

No. Online values summarize the supplied product sheet. Samples, dimensions, units, packaging, applicable standards and order terms must be confirmed before purchase.

Can plumbing products be consolidated with other categories?

Where commercially and operationally suitable, ROYAL UNION can coordinate multi-supplier receiving and consolidation as part of an agreed sourcing plan.

What should I send for a quotation?

Send the product link or photo, required dimensions, material, target quantity, destination market, packaging needs and purchasing timeline.
