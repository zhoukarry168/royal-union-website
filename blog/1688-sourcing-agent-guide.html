1688 Sourcing Agent: What International Buyers Need to Know

1688 can open the door to a broad domestic wholesale supplier base in China. The agent’s real job is to turn that access into a product, order and shipment that an international buyer can control.

ROYAL UNION Sourcing Team · Backed by MU Group · Published July 30, 2026

What is a 1688 sourcing agent?

A 1688 sourcing agent is a China-based procurement team that helps overseas buyers find products on 1688, communicate with suppliers, verify quotations, arrange samples and payments, follow production, inspect goods, consolidate orders and coordinate export delivery.

The best agent is not simply a person who places an online order. It is the operational link between a domestic wholesale platform and the buyer’s specification, quality standard, destination market and delivery plan.

What is 1688?

Alibaba Group describes 1688.com as China’s leading integrated domestic wholesale marketplace. It brings together wholesale suppliers across many industries and is widely used inside China for product and supplier discovery.

That domestic-market focus is important. Product listings, seller communication, commercial terms, payment arrangements and delivery expectations are commonly designed for buyers operating in China. An overseas buyer may find a strong product opportunity on the platform while still needing local support to confirm what is actually being offered and prepare the order for export.

A listing should therefore be treated as the beginning of a sourcing investigation—not as a complete purchasing decision.

Why do international buyers use a 1688 purchasing agent?

A buyer may be able to search 1688 independently, use translation tools and contact sellers directly. The harder work usually begins after a promising listing is found:

  • Is the seller a manufacturer, trading company, distributor or retail reseller?
  • Does the displayed price apply to the required material, size, colour, quantity and packaging?
  • Can the supplier produce consistently and support repeat orders?
  • Can the supplier meet the destination market’s labelling, testing or documentation requirements?
  • Who will receive the goods, check quantities, inspect quality and combine multiple orders?
  • How will domestic payments, transport, export documents and international delivery be coordinated?

A capable 1688 agent provides local communication and execution around these questions. This is particularly useful for e-commerce sellers, private-label brands, importers, wholesalers and retailers purchasing from several suppliers.

What should a full-service 1688 agent actually do?

The scope should be agreed before sourcing starts. For a managed purchasing programme, the work commonly includes the following stages.

1. Turn the buyer’s request into a sourcing brief

The agent should clarify the product, material, dimensions, function, target quantity, price basis, packaging, destination country and expected timing. A product photo or 1688 link alone is rarely enough to control the final order.

2. Search and compare suppliers

A useful shortlist compares more than the lowest displayed price. It should show the relevant product configuration, MOQ, sample terms, production lead time, packaging basis, customisation options and the supplier’s ability to support the programme.

3. Check the supplier and commercial offer

The agent can review available business information, clarify whether the seller is a factory or intermediary, confirm product capability and record the commercial terms. Higher-risk or higher-value projects may require additional verification or an on-site assessment.

4. Arrange and evaluate samples

Samples help confirm appearance, dimensions, materials, function and packaging before a production order. The approved sample and written specification should be kept as the reference for later quality checks.

5. Coordinate product and packaging development

For private-label orders, the work may include logo placement, colour matching, packaging artwork, carton marks, labels, manuals and barcodes. These details should be approved before mass production rather than resolved at the end.

6. Manage the purchase order and payment milestones

The agent should document supplier, product, quantity, unit price, specification, packaging, lead time and payment terms. Buyers should understand who receives funds, how supplier payments are recorded and what happens if the order changes.

7. Follow production and resolve exceptions

Order management means checking whether production is moving against the agreed plan, escalating delays and recording changes. A strong process prevents small misunderstandings from reaching the final shipment.

8. Coordinate quality control

The inspection plan should match the product and risk. It may include sample approval, material or packaging checks, in-process follow-up and pre-shipment inspection. Inspection criteria, sampling method and acceptable results should be clear before the check begins.

9. Receive and consolidate goods

When several 1688 suppliers are involved, the agent can receive cartons at a China warehouse, reconcile quantities, report visible damage and prepare a combined shipment. Consolidation can simplify export movement, but handling and storage terms should be transparent.

10. Coordinate export and international delivery

Domestic wholesale purchasing and international shipping are different stages. The final plan may require export documentation, carton and packing information, freight booking and coordination with the buyer’s customs or logistics partners.

A practical 1688 sourcing workflow

  1. Send the brief. Share product links or photos, specifications, quantity, destination market and timing.
  2. Receive a shortlist. Compare products and suppliers on the same commercial basis.
  3. Approve samples. Confirm the physical reference and record required changes.
  4. Confirm the order. Lock specification, packaging, price, MOQ, lead time and quality criteria.
  5. Follow production. Track milestones and resolve changes with the supplier.
  6. Inspect the goods. Check the order against the approved requirements before shipment.
  7. Consolidate and ship. Receive supplier orders, organise the cargo and coordinate the next delivery stage.

There is no universal 1688 agent fee. The cost depends on the service scope, product complexity, number of suppliers, order value, inspection needs, storage time and shipping plan. Buyers may encounter a percentage-based service fee, a fixed project fee or separately priced services.

Is 1688 always cheaper than Alibaba.com?

No. A 1688 listing can show a competitive domestic wholesale price, but the number may apply to a specific quantity, configuration or packaging basis. The better comparison is the total cost of an approved, export-ready order.

  • Samples, development and packaging.
  • Agent or project-management service.
  • Inspection, testing and corrective work.
  • Domestic collection, warehouse handling and consolidation.
  • Export preparation, freight, duties and destination charges.
  • The commercial cost of delays, quality failures or missed selling windows.

How to choose a reliable 1688 agent in China

A professional presentation is not enough. Ask operational questions and look for clear, written answers.

  1. Is the company legally identifiable? Confirm the contracting company, business address, payment recipient and responsible team.
  2. Is the pricing model transparent? Understand the service fee and which activities are charged separately.
  3. Will you receive a comparable quotation? Products should be compared against the same specification, quantity and packaging basis.
  4. How are suppliers checked? Ask what “verification” includes and whether factory visits are available when necessary.
  5. How are samples and approvals recorded? There should be a clear reference before production.
  6. What does quality inspection cover? Confirm the written criteria, timing, report format and reinspection process.
  7. Can the team manage multiple suppliers? Review receiving, storage, consolidation and quantity-control procedures.
  8. How are problems escalated? Ask who owns supplier delays, defects and order changes.
  9. Can the agent support your destination market? The team should collect your compliance, labelling and logistics requirements at the start.

Be cautious if an agent avoids written quotations, cannot explain its inspection scope, changes payment recipients without clear documentation or promises that every order will be risk-free. Good procurement reduces risk through process; it does not pretend risk does not exist.

What should you send before requesting a 1688 quote?

A useful sourcing brief includes:

  • 1688 product link, product name, photos or reference sample.
  • Required material, dimensions, function, colour and variants.
  • Target quantity and expected repeat-order volume.
  • Target price or acceptable commercial range.
  • Logo, packaging, barcode, label and carton requirements.
  • Destination country and applicable compliance requirements.
  • Required delivery date and preferred shipping method.
  • Any inspection criteria that are critical to acceptance.

The clearer the brief, the more meaningful the supplier comparison will be. If a requirement is not written down, it is difficult to control later.

1688 sourcing agent FAQ

What is a 1688 sourcing agent?

A 1688 sourcing agent is a China-based procurement team that helps an overseas buyer search 1688, communicate with sellers, check suppliers, confirm quotations and samples, follow orders, inspect goods, consolidate purchases and coordinate export delivery.

Can international buyers purchase directly from 1688?

Some international buyers can arrange direct purchasing, but account access, Chinese-language communication, domestic payment and delivery, supplier follow-up and export preparation can create practical challenges. Available options can vary by account, seller and destination.

Is 1688 always cheaper than Alibaba.com?

No. Compare the confirmed product specification and quantity together with samples, service, inspection, domestic transport, consolidation, export and international freight costs.

How does a 1688 agent check a supplier?

An agent can review the seller profile and business information, confirm whether the seller is a manufacturer or trading company, check product and production capability, arrange samples and document commercial terms. Agree the required depth of verification before the order.

Can a 1688 sourcing agent arrange quality inspection?

Yes. Depending on the product and order, the process can include sample approval, pre-production confirmation, in-process checks or pre-shipment inspection. The inspection scope and acceptance criteria should be written into the buying plan.

Can products from different 1688 suppliers be consolidated?

Yes. A sourcing team can receive goods from multiple suppliers, reconcile quantities, organise cartons and prepare a consolidated shipment. Confirm storage, handling and consolidation charges in advance.

What information should I send to a 1688 purchasing agent?

Send the product name or link, photos, specification, quantity, target price, packaging needs, destination market and required timeline. Include applicable compliance or labelling requirements at the beginning.
