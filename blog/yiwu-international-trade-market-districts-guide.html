Yiwu International Trade Market Districts: A Buyer Guide to Districts 1–6

A practical starting map for international buyers: find the right district for your category, then turn product discovery into a comparable supplier, sample and order plan.

ROYAL UNION Sourcing Team · Backed by MU Group · Published September 28, 2026

The short answer

The Yiwu International Trade Market is organized across specialist districts. Buyers can begin with District 1 for toys and crafts, District 2 for hardware and electrical items, District 3 for stationery and beauty accessories, District 4 for apparel accessories, District 5 for mixed lifestyle and automotive categories, and District 6 for newer fashion, children, health and smart-product categories.

The district is only a starting point. A sourcing decision still needs a clear product brief, supplier comparison, sample approval and agreed order controls.

Yiwu Market District 1: toys, flowers, jewellery and crafts

Best starting point for: toys, artificial flowers and accessories, hair ornaments, jewellery, festival crafts, decorative crafts, ceramic and crystal items, tourism crafts, photo frames and jewellery accessories.

District 1 is often relevant to buyers building gift, toy, seasonal decoration or accessory ranges. Its East section is principally jewellery and jewellery-accessory focused. When comparing novelty or seasonal products, distinguish between display samples, order-ready versions, pack size and repeat availability.

Yiwu Market District 2: bags, umbrellas, hardware and electrical products

Best starting point for: rainwear, umbrellas, suitcases and bags; locks, hardware tools and accessories; vehicle-related products; kitchen and sanitary hardware; small appliances, telecommunications, clocks, watches, electronics, batteries and lighting items.

District 2-East lists home-decoration hardware, new-energy products and intelligent hardware/electrical appliances. For tools or electrical products, write down the intended application, material, size, voltage or connection information where relevant, packaging, target market and any known document requirement before comparing suppliers.

Yiwu Market District 3: stationery, sports, beauty and accessories

Best starting point for: pen, ink and paper products; eyeglasses; office and study stationery; cultural, sports and outdoor products; cosmetics, cosmetic accessories, beauty and hairdressing supplies; mirrors, combs, buttons, zippers and apparel accessories.

District 3 is useful for stationery and beauty-accessory programmes, but a similar-looking item can vary in material, refill, artwork, packaging, set content or retail label. Ask suppliers to quote the same written requirement rather than comparing an unstructured collection of prices.

Yiwu Market District 4: socks, apparel accessories and packaging

Best starting point for: socks and leggings; knitted and cotton goods; hats, caps, gloves and earmuffs; wool, ties, lace, towels, thread and ribbon; shoes; belts; bras and underwear; scarves; and packaging boxes or bags.

For fashion-accessory orders, define material composition, size range, colour standard, logo or artwork, individual packaging, carton information, quantity by SKU and required delivery window. These choices can change both product cost and shipping volume.

Yiwu Market District 5: home living, wedding, textiles, pet and automotive

Best starting point for: general merchandise, jewellery and handicrafts, imported goods; bedding, wedding supplies, hair products and pet/aquarium supplies; curtain and knitted cloth, hotel supplies; automotive supplies and auto/motorcycle parts.

The district also includes home-living and pet-friendly areas. It can be useful for a mixed retail range, but multi-category buying requires a plan for supplier-specific MOQ, cartons, warehouse receiving and consolidation.

Yiwu Market District 6: fashion, children, health and smart products

Best starting point for: fashion fabrics, toys and fashion jewellery; apparel fabrics, baby and children's products, skin-care and medical-aesthetic products; home and medical products, intelligent equipment, travel products, popular IP collections and smart gadgets.

District 6 categories are evolving. Confirm the exact supplier, shop location, product version and intended market requirement before arranging a visit, sample or commercial order. For regulated or branded/IP-related goods, the buyer should obtain appropriate specialist advice and verify rights and market obligations.

How to use the map without wasting a buying day

  1. Start with the product, not the building. Prepare photos, dimensions, material, target price basis, quantity, packaging and destination.
  2. Choose one or two priority districts. A broad category visit is less useful than comparing a focused product requirement.
  3. Record supplier evidence. Note supplier name, shop location, contact, product model, material, MOQ, quote basis, sample terms, packaging and stated lead time.
  4. Separate discovery from approval. Market samples and verbal quotes are a starting point; confirm the final version and commercial terms in writing.
  5. Plan the China-side follow-up. Decide who will handle samples, quality criteria, receiving, consolidation and export preparation.

Buyer questions

Which Yiwu district is best for toys?

District 1 is a key starting point for toys, while District 6 also lists toys among its current product categories. Confirm the exact supplier location for the product you need.

Which district has hardware and tools?

District 2 is a key starting point for hardware, tools and electrical products. District 2-East also lists hardware and electrical specialisms.

Which district has stationery and beauty products?

District 3 is a key starting point for stationery, cultural and sports items, cosmetics, cosmetic accessories and beauty supplies.

Can a Yiwu sourcing agent arrange supplier follow-up?

Yes, when it is in the agreed scope. A sourcing partner can coordinate research, quotations, samples, order follow-up, quality checks, receiving, consolidation and export preparation.

District category information was reviewed against the Yiwugo market guide on September 28, 2026. Layouts and supplier product mixes can change; confirm the specific category, supplier location and terms before travel or purchase.
