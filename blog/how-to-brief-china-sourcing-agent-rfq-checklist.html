How to Brief a China Sourcing Agent: RFQ Checklist for Comparable Quotes

A sourcing agent cannot compare the right suppliers when each supplier is answering a different question. Start with one written brief that defines the product, quantity, quality evidence, packaging and destination.

ROYAL UNION Sourcing Team · Backed by MU Group · Published August 28, 2026

The short answer

A useful China sourcing agent brief answers twelve questions before supplier comparison begins: what the product is, how it will be used, which specification matters, expected quantity, destination, sales channel, target cost basis, packaging, branding, compliance evidence, sample timing and required shipment timing.

The purpose is not paperwork. It is to make suppliers quote the same commercial requirement. When the specification is incomplete, a low price may represent a lighter material, different component, retail pack instead of export carton, or an assumption that will change after sampling.

Why “send me your best price” produces weak quotations

A product photo can start a search, but it rarely defines a purchase. Two visually similar items can differ in material grade, thickness, dimensions, accessories, finish, packaging or intended use. The difference may only become visible after a sample arrives—or after production.

A sourcing agent should turn the initial reference into written comparison fields. The buyer still owns the commercial decision and final approval. The agent's role is to identify unanswered questions, collect evidence and keep the approved requirement connected to the order.

The 12-field China sourcing brief

Brief fieldWhat to sendWhy it changes the quote
1. Product referencePhotos, links, drawings, model numbers or a physical sample.Defines the starting point and prevents a broad, unfocused catalogue search.

When a field is unknown, write “to be confirmed” instead of guessing. That makes the open decision visible and lets the sourcing team ask a focused question.

How to compare supplier RFQs on one sheet

Place suppliers in columns and the approved requirements in rows. Keep the buyer requirement as a separate reference column so a cheaper answer cannot silently replace it. Each quotation should state:

  • supplier and quoted product version;
  • material, dimensions, construction and included accessories;
  • MOQ, price tier, currency and trade term;
  • sample cost and sample timing;
  • logo, packaging and tooling assumptions;
  • production lead-time basis and quotation validity;
  • available product, testing or quality evidence;
  • China domestic delivery and export-pack assumptions;
  • deviations, exclusions and items still to be confirmed.

Mark “not confirmed” rather than treating a blank cell as approval. A complete quotation is often more useful than the lowest headline price.

What changes after the sample?

The approved sample should become a reference, not the end of the specification process. Record its model, date, appearance, measurements, packaging and any approved changes. If the buyer requests a revision, update the written requirement and obtain confirmation before production.

For a repeat order, the current approved reference, previous inspection findings and packaging version are usually more useful than restarting from an old product link.

Buyer questions

China sourcing brief FAQ

What should I send to a China sourcing agent?

Send product references, materials and dimensions, expected quantity, destination, packaging and branding, timing, and any known testing, labelling or document requirements.

Why do China supplier quotations differ so much?

Suppliers may be answering different assumptions about material, construction, components, packaging, quantities or terms. A shared comparison sheet exposes the differences.

Do I need a target price?

A target or current cost basis can help test feasibility, but it should not replace the product specification. A sourcing team can also request costed proposals when no reliable target exists.

Can an agent prepare the specification?

An agent can organize buyer references and supplier information into a usable record. The buyer should approve the final application, critical specification, appearance, packaging and acceptance criteria.

How many suppliers should be compared?

Compare enough suitable suppliers to understand realistic specification and price options. More quotations are not useful when they describe different products.

What makes an RFQ comparable?

Every supplier answers the same fields for product version, material, dimensions, components, quantity, price basis, packaging, samples, timing, quality evidence and exclusions.
