MU Group Canton Fair Guangzhou Showroom: A More Confident Way to Source in China

For overseas buyers, the Canton Fair is not only about walking booths. The stronger route is to meet suppliers, review product categories in a dedicated showroom, and leave China with a follow-up plan that protects samples, quality, timing and consolidation.

ROYAL UNION Sourcing Team · Backed by MU Group · Published October 6, 2026

Why this matters for overseas buyers

The attached MU Group showroom notice highlights a 3,000 m² showroom in Guangzhou, located at 8F, Canton Fair Plaza, with 24-hour service, meeting room and tea room support. For buyers visiting the Canton Fair, this creates a more controlled place to discuss product categories, supplier notes and the next sourcing step.

ROYAL UNION can help buyers connect this Guangzhou showroom visit with China-side follow-up: product comparison, quotation review, sample coordination, quality-check planning, warehouse receiving, consolidation and export preparation.

Why ROYAL UNION makes the visit easier to trust

A buyer needs more than a booth conversation. A trustworthy China sourcing partner should be able to show where the team works, how categories are organized and how supplier information becomes a controlled order plan. Being connected to MU Group resources gives ROYAL UNION a stronger base for buyer meetings than a one-person anonymous agent profile.

How to use the Guangzhou showroom visit

  1. Book before you arrive. Send your fair phase, travel date, product category and buying target.
  2. Bring a short product brief. Photos, links, target quantity, destination market and price range make the meeting useful.
  3. Use the showroom for comparison. Discuss category direction, product version, packing and supplier follow-up instead of only browsing.
  4. Ask for a written next step. After the meeting, confirm which items need quotes, samples, factory check, Yiwu comparison or consolidation.
  5. Continue with Yiwu sourcing when needed. If the project needs mixed-category buying, smaller assortment building or supplier alternatives, Yiwu can be added to the route.

Buyer questions

Can I meet ROYAL UNION in Guangzhou during the Canton Fair?

Yes, by appointment and subject to schedule. Send your visit date, phase, product category and buyer brief before arrival so we can coordinate the right next step.

What should I prepare before a showroom visit?

Prepare product photos or links, quantity, destination market, target price or quality level, packaging requirements and any supplier contacts collected at the fair.

How should I use a MU Group Canton Fair video before contacting ROYAL UNION?

Use the video as a starting point for a sourcing brief: phase, date, category, target quantity, destination, product photos and whether you need Guangzhou meeting support, Yiwu sourcing follow-up or both.

Can showroom products become a real purchasing order?

Yes, but only after written confirmation of specification, quote, sample, quality requirements, packaging, timing and commercial terms. Do not rely only on verbal booth discussions.

Can ROYAL UNION continue the project after the Canton Fair?

Yes. Subject to agreed scope, we can coordinate supplier follow-up, Yiwu comparison, samples, quality checks, warehouse receiving, consolidation and export handover.
