What does ROYAL UNION do?

ROYAL UNION is a Yiwu-based China sourcing and supply-chain partner. We help buyers with product sourcing, development and packaging, supplier and order management, quality assurance, consolidation and delivery coordination.

Is ROYAL UNION part of MU Group?

ROYAL UNION is backed by MU Group and combines on-the-ground Yiwu sourcing execution with supply-chain support.

Can you help with 1688 and Yiwu sourcing?

Yes. We help buyers compare appropriate China sourcing routes, including Yiwu market and 1688 research, then coordinate the selected sourcing programme.

Which buyers do you support?

We support e-commerce sellers, brands and private-label teams, retailers and chains, importers and distributors.

Can you coordinate quality checks and consolidated shipping?

Yes. Our workflow can include quality assurance, multi-supplier consolidation and international logistics coordination before goods move to the next delivery stage.
