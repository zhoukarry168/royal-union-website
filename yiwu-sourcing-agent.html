What does a Yiwu sourcing agent do?

A Yiwu sourcing agent represents the buyer on the ground in China. The agent turns a product photo, link or specification into supplier research, comparable quotations, samples, purchase orders, quality checks, warehouse receiving, consolidation and export preparation.

The value is not access to a secret supplier list. It is the ability to keep product requirements, suppliers, quality and delivery aligned when the buyer cannot manage every step in China directly.

Buyer questions

Yiwu sourcing agent FAQ

How much does a Yiwu sourcing agent charge?

Fees depend on the service scope, order value, number of suppliers, inspection needs, warehousing and logistics work. Ask for a written quotation showing product costs and service charges separately.

Can ROYAL UNION source from 1688 as well as Yiwu Market?

Yes. We can research relevant 1688 listings, Yiwu market suppliers and factories, then compare the routes against the same buyer brief.

Can you combine goods from multiple suppliers?

Yes, when consolidation is included in the agreed scope. Goods can be received at one warehouse, reconciled and prepared for a combined shipment.

Do you guarantee product quality?

No responsible agent can guarantee that every defect is impossible. We can define an agreed quality plan, coordinate checks and report findings before the agreed shipment stage.

What information is needed to start?

Send product photos or links, specifications, quantity, destination, target price, packaging needs, timing and any known compliance requirements.
