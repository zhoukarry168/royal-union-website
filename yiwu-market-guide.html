District 1

Gifts, crafts, toys and accessories.

District 1 is a strong starting point for seasonal and gift-led ranges. It is especially relevant when you are sourcing toy, craft, artificial-flower, jewellery or decorative-product programmes.

1FArtificial flowers, flower components, stuffed toys, ordinary toys, electric toys and inflatable toys.
2FJewellery and accessories, plus hair ornaments.
3FPhoto frames, tourist crafts, decorative and festive crafts, porcelain and crystals.
4FFactory outlets and category-specific supplier comparisons.
Source District 1 categories with us →

District 2

Hardware, bags, umbrellas, electronics and tools.

District 2 concentrates practical consumer goods and hardware-led categories. It is a useful route for buyers developing home, utility, travel, tool and electronics-adjacent product ranges.

1FUmbrellas, bags and suitcases, leather goods, pouches and packaging.
2FHardware tools, tool parts, components, engineering items and fittings.
3FMachinery and hardware, telecommunications equipment and small household appliances.
4F–5FElectronic products, lamps, batteries, watches, factory outlets and foreign-trade services.
Build a District 2 supplier shortlist →

District 3

Stationery, cosmetics, educational toys and fashion components.

District 3 is particularly relevant for office and school ranges, beauty accessories, cultural goods and product components. It can support both retail assortment work and more detailed private-label development.

1FPens, ink and paper products, Spring Festival pictures, wall calendars and couplets.
2FOffice supplies and stationery, sports equipment and leisure products.
3FCosmetics, mirrors and combs, buttons, zippers, clothing accessories and educational toys.
4F–5FCosmetics, cultural and sports goods, auxiliary materials, printed pictures and factory outlets.
