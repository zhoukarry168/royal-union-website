BUYER FAQ

Furniture sourcing questions.

Final specifications, commercial terms, packaging and market requirements must be confirmed for each project.

What furniture categories can ROYAL UNION source?

We can research and coordinate home, office, outdoor, storage, commercial and children's furniture based on the buyer's specification, quantity, packaging and destination market.

Can furniture be customized with a private label?

Custom materials, colours, dimensions, finishes, branding and packaging may be coordinated when supplier capability, MOQ, sample approval and commercial terms are confirmed.

How is furniture quality checked before shipment?

The inspection plan should be based on an approved sample and written criteria covering dimensions, materials, construction, appearance, function, packaging and quantity as applicable.

Can furniture orders from multiple suppliers be consolidated?

Yes. Supplier orders can be received, reconciled and consolidated when dimensions, carton data, handling requirements and the shipping plan have been confirmed.
