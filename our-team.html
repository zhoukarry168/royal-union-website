ABOUT MU GROUP

From trading roots to a
global supply-chain organization.

MU Group grew from a China export and procurement business into an international supply-chain organization serving buyers across markets and product categories.

Its trading roots began in 1999 and Market Union was formally established at the end of 2003. ROYAL UNION was founded in Yiwu on September 1, 2006 as the group’s first subsidiary. Today, ROYAL UNION connects buyers to that wider foundation through one accountable sourcing team—from product research and development to quality control, consolidation and delivery coordination.

DEVELOPMENT PATH

Built in stages.
Connected for buyers.

Each stage added more than volume: it expanded the people, locations and operating capabilities behind international orders.

  1. 1999–2006
    01

    Trading foundation

    From early procurement activity to the formal establishment of Market Union and the launch of ROYAL UNION in Yiwu.

  2. 2007–2015
    02

    Resilience and scale

    Deeper localization in Yiwu, continued growth through changing markets and an organization approaching 1,000 people.

  3. 2016–2021
    03

    Multi-city operations

    New operating capacity, a Shanghai center and stronger export performance supported a broader China sourcing network.

  4. 2022–Today
    04

    Integrated supply-chain organization

    Cross-border e-commerce, dual headquarters in Ningbo and Yiwu, China and overseas offices, and an expanding network of business divisions.

Forward plan: MU Group’s official profile lists 2026 targets of US$3.3 billion in exports, 6,000 colleagues and more than 300,000 square metres of office and warehousing space. These are stated targets—not reported final results.
