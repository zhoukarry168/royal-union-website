Direct answers

Frequently asked China sourcing questions

What services does ROYAL UNION provide?

ROYAL UNION can coordinate product and supplier research, comparable quotations, samples, private-label and packaging work, order follow-up, quality checks, warehouse receiving, multi-supplier consolidation and export preparation. The exact scope and responsibilities are confirmed for each project. Review the complete sourcing service.

Who is the service designed for?

The service is designed for e-commerce sellers, brands and private-label teams, retailers and chains, importers and distributors. It is a commercial B2B service rather than a personal-shopping or parcel-forwarding service.

Can ROYAL UNION source from Yiwu Market and 1688?

Yes. The team can research Yiwu Market, relevant 1688 listings and direct factories, then compare those routes against the same buyer brief. Platform listings are starting points; supplier identity, specification, payment, quality and export readiness still require confirmation. Read the 1688 guide and Yiwugo guide.

Can orders from several suppliers be combined?

Yes, when consolidation is included in the agreed scope. Goods can be received at one warehouse, reconciled against order records, organized by carton and prepared for a combined shipment.

How is product quality managed?

Quality work begins with written specifications and an approved reference. ROYAL UNION can coordinate check criteria, inspection scope, documented findings and issue follow-up before the agreed shipment stage. Quality checks reduce risk but cannot prove that every possible defect is absent.

Can ROYAL UNION support private label and OEM projects?

Yes. Depending on the project, the team can coordinate product specifications, colours, logo placement, packaging artwork, labels, manuals, barcodes, carton marks and approved production references.

Does ROYAL UNION issue compliance certificates?

No. ROYAL UNION can coordinate applicable supplier documents, technical records and third-party testing. Certificates, declarations and test reports are issued by the relevant supplier, laboratory or authority. Legal requirements depend on the exact product and destination market.

How much does the service cost?

There is no universal published fee. Pricing depends on the agreed scope, order value, number of suppliers, sample work, inspection requirements, warehousing and logistics coordination. Buyers should request a written quotation that separates product and service costs.

What should a buyer send to start?

Send product photos, drawings or links, expected quantity, destination country, target price, material and packaging requirements, required delivery date and any known compliance needs. This allows suppliers to be compared on the same basis.

What we state—and what we do not claim.
